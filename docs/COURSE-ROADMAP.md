# XPDevs Learn-Munhwao: Course Roadmap

## Objective

Teach English-speaking learners Munhwao, the North Korean
standard of Korean, from absolute beginner level to advanced
independent language use.

The course should teach North Korean standard spelling,
vocabulary, grammar, pronunciation and usage.

## Curriculum

| Tiers | Stage |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Absolute beginner |
| 5-8 | Elementary foundations |
| 9-12 | Elementary development |
| 13-16 | Lower intermediate |
| 17-20 | Intermediate |
| 21-22 | Advanced sentence construction |
| 23-24 | Register and reported information |
| 25-26 | Advanced reading and writing |
| 27-28 | Vocabulary and translation |
| 29-30 | Independent use and mastery |

## Skills

The complete course should develop:

- Hangul reading.
- Accurate spelling.
- Vocabulary recognition.
- Grammar comprehension.
- Sentence construction.
- Paragraph writing.
- Reading comprehension.
- Translation.
- Speaking ability.
- Listening comprehension.

## Listening limitation

This version removes bundled audio.

Consequently, it cannot independently teach or assess
listening comprehension.

Browser text-to-speech is not proof of authentic
North Korean pronunciation.

Authentic listening instruction requires appropriately
sourced North Korean speech recordings or a validated
North Korean speech model.

## Curriculum status

The original 140 lessons are retained.

The expansion adds 160 structured lesson drafts.

Those drafts require individual explanations, verified
examples, exercises, answer keys and linguistic review.

A lesson count does not establish proficiency.
