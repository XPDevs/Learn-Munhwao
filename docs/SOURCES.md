already been checked against it.

## Verification policy

For every North Korean-specific vocabulary entry, record:

1. Hangul spelling.
2. English meaning.
3. Grammatical category.
4. Usage context.
5. Register.
6. Source.
7. Known restrictions or differences in meaning.

Do not use blanket spelling substitutions to manufacture
North Korean vocabulary.

Do not assume a word used in one context is a universal
replacement for a similar word.

Do not invent pronunciation rules.

Where sources disagree, document the disagreement.

## Review status

These are research references, not an independent
linguistic endorsement of the entire course.
